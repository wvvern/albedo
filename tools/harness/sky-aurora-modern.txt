# The Aurora preset toward the north at a field of view of 70 and 30, with shots seconds apart that show the move, the GPU sky compared with the CPU bake at time 0, the GPU time of the sharp sky, and the baked sky. Run it after scene-modern.txt:
# ./tools/screenshots.ps1 -Targets neoforge-1.21.1,neoforge-1.21.11 -Shots sky-aurora-modern.txt -OutDir build\sky-aurora
