# Dusk, the generators of Galaxy and Nebula, the full Galaxy and Nebula with their filters, and each other filter over Dusk,
# with the Sharp render on and off at a field of view of 30 and 70, each with the GPU sky compared with the CPU bake and the
# GPU time of the sharp sky. Run it after the scene:
# python -m tools harness fabric-26.3 fabric-1.21.11 fabric-1.21.1 forge-1.8.9 --suite sky-sharp --out build/sky-sharp
wait 60
profile minimal
run time set 6000
wait 40
sky dusk
wait 160
# The wall of glass and leaves of the scene in front of the sharp sky and of the baked sky, with the time of the sky held.
skyclock 0
fov 70
run tp @p ~ ~ ~ 0 -20
wait 40
screenshot wall-01-sharp-fov70
skyrender fast
wait 160
screenshot wall-02-baked-fov70
skyrender sharp
skyclock run
wait 160
# Yaw 90 looks west (the Dusk glow), pitch -60 looks up to the stars.

wait 40
screenshot sharp-02-on-west-fov30

wait 40

wait 40
