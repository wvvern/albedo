# Moving Chroma colors on the GPU: frames in a row within one tick, a longer row of frames, far blocks with mip levels,
# the GPU colors at the time 0 against the still colors of the CPU, the GPU time of the pass, and the Effects section.
# Run it after scene-*.txt in place of shots.txt:
# ./tools/screenshots.ps1 -Targets fabric-26.3,forge-1.8.9 -Shots chroma-modern.txt -OutDir build\chroma
wait 60
# The pastel profile keeps the light and dark pattern of the textures, so that a screenshot has many colors to measure.
profile pastel
effects blocks chroma 100 speed 100
effects fluids chroma 100 speed 100
effects items chroma 100 speed 100
wait 100
# Six frames in a row, which the log gives with the time of the colors of each frame.
frames 6 c01-burst
wait 20
# Thirty frames in a row, for the change of the hue from frame to frame.
frames 30 c02-row
wait 20
# Far grass to the horizon, where the atlas shows its mip levels.
run tp @p 0.5 -58 4.5 180 4
wait 40
frames 6 c03-far
wait 20
run tp @p 0.5 -60 4.5 180 12
wait 100
# The GPU colors at the time 0 are the still colors that the CPU paints when the pass stops.
skyclock 0
wait 10
screenshot c04-gpu-time0
chromagpu off
wait 60
screenshot c05-cpu-still
chromagpu on
wait 60
screenshot c06-gpu-again
skyclock run
chromatime 300
wait 200
# A repaint while the colors move: Bevel and Outline stack on Chroma.
effects blocks chroma 100 speed 100 bevel 50 outline 50
wait 60
frames 3 c07-bevel-outline
wait 20
# A resource reload makes a new atlas, and the colors move again on it.
reload
wait 300
frames 3 c08-after-reload
wait 20
effects blocks chroma 100 speed 30 saturation 80 brightness 40
wait 20
settings open
wait 20
ui click Effects
wait 10
ui pointer off
wait 10
screenshot c09-settings-effects
settings close
wait 10
quit
