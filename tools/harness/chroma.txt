# Moving Chroma colors: screenshots two seconds apart, the "Chroma tick" times in the log, and the Effects section with

# ./tools/screenshots.ps1 -Targets fabric-26.3,forge-1.8.9 -Shots chroma.txt -OutDir build\chroma
